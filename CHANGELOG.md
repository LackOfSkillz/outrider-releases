# Changelog

## 0.4.6 (2026-11-23)

- **Gear sources: 11,164 items** (was 8,909). Dungeon bosses now point to the dungeon's entrance ("Lady Anacondra (Wailing Caverns, 37%)"), rares and quest givers use every Forever map spot ForeverDB has, quest rewards are named even when the giver's spot isn't known, and PvP vendors are named. GearQuest now knows 1,104 items we don't, and 704 of those have no source there either.

## 0.4.5 (2026-11-23)

- **Crafting knows what every recipe makes:** 2,329 recipes from WoW Forever's own client tables (what each makes, the skill to learn it, when it turns yellow and grey, and its reagents), and how each is learned from AllTheThings (trainer, recipe item or quest). The Professions page lists **Recipes to learn next** for your skill, with where to get each pattern (the vendor or the creature that drops it, and how far) and a Go button. The Gear page now includes crafted gear (1,351 items) when you have the profession: "made with Tailoring (skill 75)".
- **Windows always fit the screen:** with Larger windows on, the main window was scaled past the bottom of the screen, resize grip and all. It now shrinks to fit, any other window that's too big is scaled down, and one hanging off an edge comes back to the middle.
- **Smarter trip planner:** flights follow the real flight legs between flight points you know, with their real flight times (no more straight-line guesses, and no flight between two points that aren't connected). Boats and zeppelins use their timetables: the time counts the average wait at the dock plus the crossing, and the step says how often it leaves and how long the trip takes. Exact dock spots, the Darnassus portals and the Thunder Bluff lifts are in too. All from Shortest Path Forever via ForeverDB (GPLv3), in a new data addon, Outrider_Data_Travel.
- **Herbalism page filled in:** "How to raise it" was empty (two empty lists sat on top of the text). It now says which herbs fit your skill and lists each with its closest spot and a Go button, newest herb first.
- **Gear page stands on its own without GearQuest:** when your zone has few upgrades, the best one per slot from other zones is added (your continent first), each with where it is and a waypoint. No pop-up for those.
- **Far more gear knows where it comes from:** 8,909 items (was 2,954), from QuestieDB plus ForeverDB (a GPLv3 merge of WoW Forever's own client tables, AllTheThings, VMaNGOS, cMaNGOS and loot counts recorded on Forever). New: dungeon and raid boss drops (the waypoint is the entrance), rare creatures, chests, world drops ("world drop from creatures level 38 and up"), drop chances on the card ("Defias Pillager (2.4%)"), and Forever's own vendors and quest rewards. Gear you can craft with your own learned recipes is listed too ("you can make it with Tailoring").
- **NPC spots trued up to WoW Forever:** vendors, trainers and quest givers use ForeverDB's Forever positions where they differ (95 moved, 7 added). Checked against 203 spots Outrider recorded in game: 3%+ off went from 4 to 2.
- **The way-there dots only follow verified ground:** dots are drawn only on ground someone really walked (your trails, shipped trails, creature patrols), and only when it reaches the destination. No more straight lines across hills and cliffs; with no walked way yet there are no dots and the arrow still points. The dots are bigger with a dark rim, so they show on any ground.

## 0.4.4 (2026-11-23)

- **Unexplored map areas** show with a faint blue-grey tint, so you can tell them from land you've explored (they looked the same).
- **Area level numbers** draw above the map's explored-area art, which hid them.
- **Map level numbers are back:** a zone's level range is its own line under the name again (one string with the name, a line break and the range drew only the name), and Outrider's own data addons that the game had switched off for a character are switched back on, so the creature pack loads and each area gets its range. The map logs what it draws per map.
- **Resale can't suggest a losing buy:** the price to sell again is now just under the cheapest seller left after you buy, not the old "usual" price. A market that dropped (Thorium Headed Arrows: 79c on earlier days, 35c today with 3,400 listed at 35c) is no longer a deal, and buying only part of a cheap listing never is, since the rest still sells at that price. The steps say why ("just under the cheapest other seller").
- **Talents page totals:** each talent's rank is counted once (a Balance build said "+74% less Mana cost": Moonglow's 9% at 3/3 was multiplied by 3 again), and a number is only read from its own sentence. What each talent's text says is logged once a session so the totals can be checked against the game.

## 0.4.3 (2026-11-23)

- **Talents page** (Class, Talents), on one screen: the builds on the left, your three trees in the middle with the links between talents, what the build gives on the right.
  - 57 researched builds for every class (levelling, max damage, max healing, tank, PvP), each with its split and where it came from.
  - Try a build, change any point with the game's rules (5 points a row, links, points for your level), slide the level to see it as you grow, and see what to learn or take off from your build.
  - What a build gives, added up from the talents' own text (crit, hit, stats, damage, healing), at levels 20 to 60, and your stats with it.
  - An estimate of damage or healing per second for each build from your spells' tooltips (toughness for tanks); the best is marked "Outrider's pick for you".
  - Training dummy: hit one for 10 seconds or more and your real damage per second for your build is recorded from Blizzard's meter.
  - Follow a build: the game's talent window rings your next points. Share builds with codes.
- **Class page, every class:** "Spells to learn" checks this character's spellbook (it showed spells already learned, each twice); talent points are read from the game's talent system (it said 0); a "Do this now" list at the top; heal bars take less room.
- **Pull key** for druids, shamans, priests, mages and warlocks: your ranged weapon if you have one (a wand shoots), otherwise your class's opener (Moonfire, Lightning Bolt, Shadow Word: Pain, Frostbolt, Immolate). Warriors and rogues keep theirs.
- **Fight keys swing too:** caster fight keys start your auto attack, so when the mob reaches you you hit it without clicking it again (not with a wand, which melee would stop).
- **Druid Strike** waits for Bear or Cat Form; before that it was a copy of Fight.
- **Macros that stopped updating:** a copy the game hands back with only Outrider's own lines (Sheer's Fight lost its Thorns line) is Outrider's again and gets updated and dragged; an edited macro still drags as you wrote it.

Auction deals:

## 0.4.0 (2026-11-22)

Everything Auctionator does, inside Outrider's shopping window at the auction house:

- **Sell bags:**
  - Everything in your bags that can be sold, one card per item, priced just under the lowest price someone else asks right now.
  - One click on **Post** puts it up: stackable goods all at once (Ctrl-click: one stack), gear one at a time.
  - A price under what a vendor pays, or under half the usual price, needs a second click.
  - Click a card to type your own price; it's kept for that item.
  - Right-click hides an item from the list.
  - Chips for 12, 24 or 48 hours.
- **My auctions:**
  - Your auctions with the lowest price now; undercut ones are marked and sorted to the top.
  - **Cancel** takes one down, one click each, and **Cancel next undercut** does the next one.
- **Keys you can bind (Key Bindings, AddOns, Outrider):** post the top item, cancel the next undercut auction.
- **Search:**
  - Search for anything, with plain extras: "exact name" in quotes, lvl 10-20, under 5g (Buy never pays more), green+, usable, x20.
  - Recent searches are kept.
- **Shopping lists:**
  - Save searches into named lists and search a whole list in one go.
  - Import Auctionator lists, TSM item lists or plain names; export to share.
- **Tooltips:**
  - The price of the whole stack under your mouse.
  - What disenchanting green and blue gear is likely worth (an estimate from the classic tables and auction prices).
- **Price scan when the auction house opens:** `/ride ahsell autoscan on` (once every 15 minutes).
- **Smoother auction house:**
  - The shopping window no longer lags the game (it dropped to 2 FPS). Vendor flips and Resale are worked out once per price scan, and bag sizes are read once.
  - The separate undercut box beside the auction house is gone: My auctions has it.
- **No test popups at login:** the "Wave" button and sample alert no longer show on a new install.
